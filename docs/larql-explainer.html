How to read this: The left half (tokens, embedding, attention, probabilities) is identical to the standard transformer pipeline shown in the Transformer Explainer. The middle MLP panel is what LARQL reorganizes: instead of a dense up→GeGLU→down matmul, the 10,240 intermediate neurons become a queryable feature index. The forward pass becomes a walk: KNN over gate vectors finds the top-K nodes that match the residual x, only those features fire, and only their down vectors get summed back. Each layer has its own independent feature graph; on Gemma-3 4B that's 34 graphs of ~10,240 nodes each. Numbers shown are illustrative — drag the Layer and K sliders to see the walk change.
